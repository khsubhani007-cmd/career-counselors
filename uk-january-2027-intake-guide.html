UK January 2027 Intake: Complete Guide for Pakistani Students

Everything Pakistani students need for the UK January 2027 intake: universities, English requirements, CAS, financial documents, TB test, Graduate Route and timelines.

Career Counselors TeamStudy abroad consultants, DHA Lahore
18 October 20267 min read

Key takeaways

  • Many UK universities run January starts, especially for one-year master's.
  • You need an offer, CAS, funds held for the required period and a TB test.
  • Eligible graduates can apply for the Graduate Route after their degree.

Missed September? Many UK universities offer a January intake, especially for master's programmes in business, computing, engineering and health. Here is how to prepare for January 2027.

Why choose the January intake?

  • More time to prepare documents and finances
  • Many one-year master's programmes start in January
  • Smaller class sizes on some courses
  • The same degree and visa rights as September

Step-by-step process

  1. 1
    Shortlist universities that run your course in January.
  2. 2
    Apply with transcripts, SOP, CV and references.
  3. 3
    Accept your offer and pay any deposit the university requires.
  4. 4
    Prepare finances. UK rules require funds to be held for a set period before you apply.
  5. 5
    Get your TB test at an approved clinic. This is required for Pakistani applicants.
  6. 6
    Receive your CAS (Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies) from the university.
  7. 7
    Apply for the Student visa and attend your biometrics appointment.
💡
Counselor tip

January deadlines arrive fast. Start your applications and finances as early as possible so your CAS and visa are ready in time.

English requirements

Most universities ask for IELTS (or UKVI IELTS for some courses). Some accept previous English-medium study or other tests. Ask us which universities fit your English background.

After graduation

Eligible graduates can apply for the Graduate Route to stay and work in the UK after their degree. The length of stay is set by UK government rules, which can change, so we confirm the current rules during counselling.

Frequently asked questions

Is the January intake good for Pakistani students?
Yes. Many universities offer the same courses and the degree, visa and post-study rights are the same as September.
Do I need a TB test for the UK?
Yes. Applicants from Pakistan need a TB test certificate from an approved clinic for UK visas longer than six months.
