Student Visa Documents Checklist for Pakistani Students

A clear checklist of the documents Pakistani students usually need for UK, Ireland, Italy, Hungary and Cyprus student visas, plus tips to avoid delays and refusals.

Career Counselors Team18 October 2026 · 5 min read
🛂
In this article
  1. Core documents
  2. Country-specific extras
  3. Get your file checked

Every country has its own rules, but most student visa files are built from the same core documents. Start collecting these early. Missing or inconsistent papers are the most common cause of delays.

Core documents

  • Valid passport (with enough validity for your course)
  • Admission or offer letter (CAS for the UK, pre-enrolment for Italy)
  • Academic transcripts and degrees (attested where required)
  • English proficiency proof (IELTS, other test or MOI letter)
  • Statement of Purpose and CV
  • Financial proof: bank statements, sponsor letters and source of funds
  • Passport-size photos to the required specification

Country-specific extras

  • UK: TB test certificate and funds held for the required period
  • Ireland: medical insurance and proof of fee payment
  • Italy: Universitaly pre-enrolment, accommodation proof and translated documents
  • Hungary & Cyprus: accommodation proof, medical certificates as required
💡

Keep every document consistent: the same spelling of your name, the same dates and the same figures everywhere.

Frequently asked questions

How much bank balance do I need for a student visa?
It depends on the country, city and course. Each country publishes its own financial requirement, which we confirm for your case.
Do I need documents attested?
Many countries require attested or legalised academic documents. Requirements differ, so check before you apply.

This guide is general information, not legal or immigration advice. Rules, fees and deadlines change. Always check the official university, scholarship and embassy sources, or ask our counselors for your case.
