Stipendium Hungaricum Scholarship: Guide for Pakistani Students

How Pakistani students can apply for the Stipendium Hungaricum scholarship to study in Hungary: what it covers, eligibility, the two-step application and tips.

Career Counselors TeamStudy abroad consultants, DHA Lahore
18 October 20266 min read

Key takeaways

  • Government scholarship covering tuition, a monthly stipend, housing support and insurance.
  • Pakistani applicants apply online and also need HEC nomination.
  • Deadlines usually fall in the winter months for a September start.

  1. What the scholarship usually covers
  2. How the application works

The Stipendium Hungaricum is a scholarship programme run by the Hungarian government for students from partner countries, including Pakistan. It is one of the most generous fully-funded options in Europe.

What the scholarship usually covers

  • Tuition-free study at a Hungarian university
  • A monthly stipend
  • Accommodation in a dormitory or a housing contribution
  • Medical insurance

How the application works

There are usually two parts:

  1. 1
    Online application on the Stipendium Hungaricum portal, where you choose programmes and upload documents.
  2. 2
    Nomination by Pakistan's sending partner (for Pakistan, the Higher Education Commission), which has its own requirements and deadline.

Shortlisted applicants may then be interviewed or tested by the universities.

💡
Counselor tip

Applications for September usually open in autumn and close in the winter months. Check both the Stipendium Hungaricum and HEC deadlines, because missing either one ends your application.

Not selected? Hungary is still affordable

Even as a self-funded student, Hungary's tuition and living costs are lower than most of Western Europe, and you live in the Schengen area. Some universities also offer their own discounts.

Frequently asked questions

Can Pakistani students apply for Stipendium Hungaricum?
Yes. Pakistan is a partner country. Applicants also need nomination through Pakistan's sending partner, the Higher Education Commission.
Is Hungary good for Pakistani students?
Hungary offers EU degrees, English-taught programmes and lower costs than most Western European countries.
