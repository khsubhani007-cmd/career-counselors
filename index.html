FAQ

Questions Students Ask Us

Which countries can I apply to through Career Counselors?
We guide students for the United Kingdom, Ireland, Italy, Hungary and Cyprus, with January 2027 and September 2027 intakes.
Can I study abroad without IELTS?
Often, yes. Several universities in the UK, Italy, Hungary and Cyprus accept a Medium of Instruction (MOI) letter or other English proof. Requirements depend on the university, so we check your profile first.
Are scholarships available for Pakistani students?
Yes — from university merit scholarships to regional scholarships in Italy that can cover full tuition and pay a stipend for eligible students.
Where is your office?
Our office is at the DHA Branch, Lahore. You can also get counselling on WhatsApp or by phone at +92 324 9499994.
How do I start?
Fill in the consultation form or message us on WhatsApp. A counselor will review your profile and suggest the best options.
