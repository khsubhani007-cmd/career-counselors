Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-18

This policy explains how Career Counselors ("we", "us") handles information you share through this website.

Information we collect

When you use our consultation form, you provide details such as your name, phone/WhatsApp number, city, preferred destination, qualification and English test status. The form sends this information to us through WhatsApp; it is not stored on this website.

We also use cookies and similar technologies from Google (such as Google Ads and Google Analytics) to measure visits and the performance of our advertising.

How we use it

  • To contact you about study abroad options you asked about
  • To prepare university and visa applications at your request
  • To improve our website and advertising

Sharing

We share your documents with universities, scholarship bodies or visa authorities only when you ask us to apply on your behalf. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete your information at any time by contacting us at +92 324 9499994. You can manage Google ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com.
